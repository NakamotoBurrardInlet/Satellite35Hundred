1. Simplified General Perturbations (SGP4 / SDP4) Propagator

Unlike idealized two-body Keplerian motion governed strictly by $\ddot{\mathbf{r}} = -\frac{\mu}{r^3}\mathbf{r}$, artificial Earth satellites experience secular and periodic orbital perturbations. This system utilizes satellite.js implementing the NORAD SGP4/SDP4 analytical perturbation model. SGP4 accounts for Earth's oblateness zonal harmonics ($J_2, J_3, J_4$) which induce nodal regression ($\dot{\Omega}$) and apsidal precession ($\dot{\omega}$), atmospheric drag modeled via the ballistic coefficient $B^*$, and deep-space lunar-solar gravitational resonance for MEO/GEO regimes.

2. Reference Frame Transformations: TEME → ECEF → WGS-84 Geodetic

At each 11-second telemetry epoch $t$, SGP4 outputs state vectors $\mathbf{r}_{\text{TEME}}$ and $\mathbf{v}_{\text{TEME}}$ in the True Equator, Mean Equinox (TEME) inertial frame. To project each spacecraft accurately onto the rotating spherical Earth grid, the engine computes Greenwich Mean Sidereal Time ($\theta_{\text{GMST}}$) and rotates coordinates into Earth-Centered, Earth-Fixed (ECEF) space before solving Bowring's iterative geodetic transformation for WGS-84 latitude ($\phi$), longitude ($\lambda$), and ellipsoidal altitude ($h$).
